How Lease Options Work

A lease option combines a standard rental agreement with the right to purchase the property at a predetermined price within a specified timeframe. The tenant-buyer pays option consideration upfront and commits to monthly rent, with a portion often credited toward the eventual purchase.

This structure provides owners with consistent cash flow and downside protection while giving qualified tenant-buyers time to improve credit, save for closing costs, and lock in a purchase price.

Agreement Execution

Owner and tenant-buyer enter a lease with an option to purchase at a set price and term

Occupancy Period

Tenant-buyer occupies the property, pays monthly rent, and works toward purchase readiness

Option Exercise

If qualified, tenant-buyer exercises the option and closes on the property purchase
