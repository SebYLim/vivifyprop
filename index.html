How Lease Options Work

Structured Lease

The occupant enters into a standard residential lease with clearly defined rent, term, and responsibilities. The lease stands on its own and is enforceable independent of any future option.

Separate Purchase Option

A separate option agreement may grant the tenant the right—but not the obligation—to purchase the property at a defined price and timeframe, subject to strict terms.

Active Management

We manage rent collection, compliance tracking, maintenance coordination, and communication to reduce ambiguity and protect the owner’s position throughout the lease period.

Who We Serve

Property Owners

Owners seeking flexible exit strategies, improved cash flow stability, or alternatives to traditional leasing or immediate sale—without relinquishing control of the asset.

Tenant-Buyers

Occupants who value stability and optionality, understand the responsibilities of a lease-option structure, and are prepared to perform under clearly documented agreements.

Our Approach

Rigorous Screening

Compliance-Oriented Execution

We operate with attention to state and local landlord-tenant regulations and coordinate with legal counsel when structuring or modifying agreements.

For Property Owners

Vivify works with a limited number of property owners seeking structured alternatives to traditional leasing or immediate sale through properly managed lease option arrangements.

What We Do — Clearly

We manage residential properties that utilize lease options as an operating structure. The property remains owned by you. The lease governs occupancy. Any purchase option is separate, conditional, and never guaranteed.

Why Owners Use Lease Options

Lease options can provide flexibility where traditional leasing or selling is suboptimal. Common owner motivations include vacancy reduction, improved tenant commitment, pricing optionality, and time-based exit planning.

Important: Lease options are not sales contracts. They involve risk, require careful documentation, and are not appropriate for every property or owner.

Our Management Philosophy

We approach lease options conservatively. Screening standards are higher than typical rentals. Documentation is explicit. Expectations are documented. We prioritize enforceability and downside protection over volume.

What We Are Not

We are not marketers selling homeownership promises. We do not guarantee outcomes. We do not blur the line between leasing and selling. Our role is operational management and risk control.
