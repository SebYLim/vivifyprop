For Property Owners

Vivify works with a limited number of property owners seeking structured alternatives to traditional leasing or immediate sale through properly managed lease option arrangements.

What We Do — Clearly

We manage residential properties that utilize lease options as an operating structure. The property remains owned by you. The lease governs occupancy. Any purchase option is separate, conditional, and never guaranteed.

Why Owners Use Lease Options

Lease options can provide flexibility where traditional leasing or selling is suboptimal. Common motivations include vacancy reduction, improved occupant commitment, pricing optionality, and time-based exit planning.

Important: Lease options are not sales contracts. They involve risk, require careful documentation, and are not appropriate for every property or owner.

Our Management Philosophy

We approach lease options conservatively. Screening standards exceed typical rentals. Documentation is explicit. Expectations are documented. We prioritize enforceability and owner protection over transaction volume.

What We Are Not

We are not marketers selling homeownership promises. We do not guarantee outcomes. We do not blur the line between leasing and selling. Our role is operational management and risk control.
